Get help

Questions, problems or ideas: open an issue (a GitHub account is needed). Please describe what you did, what you expected and your macOS version; don't attach private databases, a small made-up example is enough.

In the app, Help ▸ Rowboat Help explains each window, and Help ▸ Keyboard Shortcuts lists the shortcuts.

Common questions

Can Rowboat open a database next to its journal (-wal, -shm)? Yes; open the database file itself and macOS lets Rowboat reach the files beside it.

Where are my query tabs, history and snippets kept? On your Mac, inside Rowboat's own container. Nothing is sent anywhere.

Is there a sample to try? File ▸ Open Sample Database makes one with a million orders.
